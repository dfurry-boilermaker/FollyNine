Trading

Over the past few years, I have become fascinated with the trading world. This has led me down a path of studying the psychology of trading, risk management, and technical analysis. I have learned the most while reading books like Market Wizards by Jack D. Schwager, Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets by John J. Murphy, and Martin Zweig's Winning on Wall Street. I find myself engaged by world economics and how different forms of monetary policy can drive market movements. I am striving to improve my trade strategy and materialize my conviction continuously. noun · /ˈfälē/

Developed by Daniel Furry [danielfurry22@gmail.com]

You might wonder why anyone would choose "folly" to describe their website. Well, I'm glad you asked… Folly perfectly captures the essence of this site. In the past, folly has been a term to affectionately describe things like family cars, sailboats, and even my childhood puppy, a Black Lab Mutt with only three legs. At first glance, these things might seem silly or even foolish, as the definition suggests. But in reality, they've been the very things that have brought my family and friends together time and time again, creating unforgettable memories that we'll cherish forever.
